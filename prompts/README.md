# HP-15C prompt archive

This directory records the app-building prompts available in the conversation and the image-generation prompts preserved in the research archive. User wording is retained; presentation whitespace is normalized.

- [User build prompts](user-build-prompts.md): requirements, workspace decisions, visual feedback, and the scientific-function continuation request, in conversation order.
- [Image-generation prompts](image-generation-prompts.md): recorded keycap and LCD prompts, with reference-input context.

This is an archive of available source text, not a complete conversation export. The earlier clarification questions/proposal referenced in the user's numbered answers are not available in the retained conversation. Original research and critic-agent dispatch prompts are also not preserved verbatim here; their resulting research and reviews are in `artifacts/research/` and `artifacts/reviews/`. Missing prompts have not been reconstructed and presented as originals.

Routine usage-reset messages, status questions, calculator-use questions and repository-administration exchanges are omitted because they do not define the app's implementation. System/developer instructions and automatically supplied browser context are not user build prompts.
