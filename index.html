Memory, statistics and complex numbers

STO / RCL then 0–9 or .0–.9 stores/recalls a register. After STO/RCL, + − × ÷ performs register arithmetic. TAN selects I; COS selects the register addressed by I. f 4 exchanges X with a register. f x⇄y clears registers.

f ENTER generates a reproducible random number; STO ENTER seeds it; RCL ENTER recalls the seed. g 4 / 5 / 6 then 0–9 sets/clears/tests a flag. Flag 8 enables complex mode; flag 9 controls overflow blinking. f 5 / f 6 then a register updates DSE/ISG counters and reports the condition.

Statistics: f GSB clears statistics and stack. Enter y ENTER x Σ+ for each pair; g Σ+ removes a pair. g 0: means; g decimal: sample deviations; f Σ+: intercept in X, slope in Y; x f decimal: predicted y in X, correlation in Y. RCL Σ+ recalls sums.

Complex: enter real ENTER imaginary f TAN. f − swaps real and imaginary X; hold f COS to inspect imaginary X. g 5 8 exits complex mode. Complex trig uses radians; coordinate conversions use the selected angle unit.

Matrices A–E

After a matrix command, use the top-row keys √x, eˣ, 10ˣ, yˣ, 1/x for A–E; no extra f is needed. Enter rows ENTER columns f SIN A to dimension A. f CHS 1 initializes row/column registers; f RCL enables USER stepping. Enter each value then STO A. RCL A recalls each element. R0/R1 specify an individual cell; alternatively row ENTER column RCL g A recalls it.

RCL CHS A recalls a matrix descriptor; STO CHS B copies it to B (or fills B when X is scalar). f EEX C selects result matrix C. Arithmetic and 1/x operate on descriptors. Matrix division computes X⁻¹Y. Products require a result matrix distinct from their inputs. To allocate the maximum 64 matrix elements: 1 f SIN COS; this leaves only R0, R1 and I as storage.

f CHS digit: 0 clear matrices; 1 reset cell indices; 2 expand complex blocks; 3 reduce blocks; 4 transpose; 5 YᵀX; 6 residual RESULT−YX; 7 row norm; 8 Frobenius norm; 9 determinant (LU in RESULT). On descriptors, f + / g + pack/unpack complex elements.

Hold f ← for the full mantissa. Hold g RCL for memory allocation. Hold ON and press decimal (or hold O and press . on your keyboard) to toggle the radix convention. The controls below also include a radix-convention toggle and a readable memory inspector.

All original legends remain visible. Programming, subroutine execution and hardware diagnostics are excluded. SOLVE and integration use the function editor below. Function editor & memory inspector

Define f(x) for SOLVE and integration. Enter two root estimates, or lower then upper integration bounds, using ENTER between them. Use the gold SOLVE / ∫ keys or the buttons below. Computation can be cancelled.

Use x, pi, e, + − * / ^, parentheses, and sqrt, abs, exp, ln, log (base 10), sin/cos/tan and their inverse/hyperbolic forms, floor, ceil, int, frac, factorial, square, recip, pow10, hms, hours, rad, deg; pow(a,b), perm(n,r), comb(n,r). Write multiplication explicitly: 2*x. Real trig follows the selected angle unit. In complex mode, i and complex(real,imag) are available, trig uses radians, and SOLVE/integration sample the real part along the real x axis.
